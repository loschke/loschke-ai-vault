# Konzeption & Prototyping

Ideen sichtbar machen, bevor sie in Produktion gehen. Bildgenerierung als Thinking Tool für Designer, Marketer und Produktentwickler.

## Use Cases

### Logo-Entwicklung, Illustration & Branding
Schnelle Exploration von Richtungen, bevor der Illustrator übernimmt.

```
a minimal line art logo of a mountain with sun rays, black on white, vector style
a geometric logo mark combining a leaf and a droplet, flat design, single color
an abstract logo symbol representing connectivity and growth, modern, clean lines
```

### Corporate Design Ideen und Prototyping
Visuelle Sprache testen: Farbwelten, Bildstile, Moodboards.

```
a corporate photography style showing diverse professionals in a modern office, warm tones, authentic moments
a brand imagery concept with abstract blue gradients and geometric shapes, premium feel
a visual identity exploration combining nature elements with minimalist design, earth tones
```

### Konzepte & Ideen für Print & Magazine
Layouts visualisieren, Bildsprache definieren, Art Direction testen.

```
a magazine cover concept with bold typography overlay on abstract paint splashes, editorial style
a print advertisement layout showing a luxury watch on marble surface, high-end photography style
a brochure imagery concept with layered paper cutout effect, pastel colors, dimensional
```

### Ideen für Produktvisualisierungen
Produkte zeigen, die noch nicht existieren – oder in Kontexten, die schwer zu fotografieren sind.

```
a product visualization of a smart home device on a minimalist shelf, soft lighting, lifestyle context
a concept rendering of a sustainable packaging design, kraft paper texture, eco-friendly aesthetic
a product shot of wireless earbuds floating with dynamic light trails, tech advertising style
```

### 3D Prototyping
Räumliche Konzepte schnell visualisieren, bevor aufwändige 3D-Modelle entstehen.

```
a 3D concept render of a modular furniture system, isometric view, clean studio lighting
a product prototype visualization of an electric vehicle charging station, industrial design style
a 3D mockup of a retail kiosk design, modern materials, glass and brushed metal
```

### Ideen für Standdesign und Messen
Messeauftritte konzipieren, bevor der Standbauer kalkuliert.

```
a trade show booth concept for a tech company, open design, LED walls, meeting areas
an exhibition stand visualization with curved walls and integrated product displays, premium finish
a fair booth design featuring sustainable materials, plants, and natural wood, welcoming atmosphere
```

## Workflow-Tipp

Generierte Konzepte sind **Kommunikationsmittel**, nicht finale Designs:
- Schnell Richtungen abstimmen mit Stakeholdern
- Briefings für Illustratoren/Designer konkretisieren
- Varianten zeigen statt beschreiben
