# Stock-, Fashion-, Natur-, Produktfotografie

Fotorealistische Bilder ohne Shooting. Von Landschaften bis Porträts, von Produkten bis Details.

## Use Cases

### Outdoor & Abenteuer
Actionreiche Szenen und Naturerlebnisse.

```
a hiker standing on mountain peak at sunrise, epic landscape view, adventure photography style
a mountain biker mid-jump against dramatic sky, action frozen, sports photography
a camping scene at twilight with tent and campfire, cozy wilderness atmosphere
```

### Landschaften & Umgebung
Orte und Stimmungen für jeden Kontext.

```
a misty forest path in autumn with golden leaves, moody atmospheric photography
a dramatic coastal cliff at sunset with crashing waves, long exposure effect
an aerial view of lavender fields in Provence, purple rows stretching to horizon
```

### Personen & Porträts
Menschen in authentischen Situationen – ohne Model-Booking.

```
a portrait of a confident businesswoman in modern office, natural light, editorial style
a candid shot of friends laughing at outdoor cafe, authentic moment, lifestyle photography
a craftsman working in workshop, focused expression, dramatic side lighting
```

### Inszenieren von Details
Nahaufnahmen, die Geschichten erzählen.

```
close-up of weathered hands holding fresh vegetables, farmers market aesthetic
macro shot of water droplets on leaf surface, morning light, nature detail
detail shot of vintage watch mechanism, precision engineering, luxury feel
```

### Produktfotografie
Produkte professionell in Szene setzen.

```
a skincare product on bathroom shelf with soft morning light, lifestyle product photography
a gourmet dish beautifully plated on rustic wooden table, food photography, shallow depth of field
a pair of sneakers on urban concrete with graffiti background, streetwear aesthetic
```

### Individuelle nahtlose Muster
Fotorealistische Texturen und Wiederholungen.

```
a seamless photo texture of stacked firewood, natural pattern, tileable
a seamless pattern of scattered autumn leaves on ground, top view, natural arrangement
a seamless food pattern of sliced citrus fruits, bright and fresh, white background
```

## Fotografie-Spezifische Prompt-Elemente

**Kamera & Technik:**
- shot on Canon 5D, 85mm lens, f/1.8
- long exposure, motion blur
- shallow depth of field, bokeh
- high-speed photography, frozen action

**Licht & Stimmung:**
- golden hour, warm tones
- overcast soft light, no harsh shadows
- dramatic side lighting, chiaroscuro
- backlit, rim light, silhouette

**Stil-Referenzen:**
- editorial photography style
- National Geographic style
- lifestyle photography, candid
- commercial product photography

## Workflow-Tipp

Fotorealismus braucht **Spezifität**:
- Kamera-Settings suggerieren (auch wenn die KI keine echte Kamera nutzt)
- Lichtsituation genau beschreiben
- "photo of" oder "photograph of" als Trigger für Realismus
- Nachbearbeitung per Editing für finale Perfektion
