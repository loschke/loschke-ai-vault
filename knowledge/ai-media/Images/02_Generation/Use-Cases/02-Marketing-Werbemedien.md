# Marketing & Werbemedien

Fertige Assets für Kampagnen und Kommunikation. Schnell produzierbar, flexibel anpassbar.

## Use Cases

### Digital Mockups
Designs im Kontext zeigen – Screens, Devices, Umgebungen.

```
a smartphone mockup held in hand showing an app interface, shallow depth of field, lifestyle setting
a laptop mockup on a wooden desk with coffee and plants, cozy home office atmosphere
a website mockup displayed on multiple devices, clean white background, perspective view
```

### Produktmockups
Physische Produkte in Szene setzen, ohne Fotoshooting.

```
a packaging mockup of a skincare bottle on stone surface with water droplets, spa atmosphere
a book cover mockup lying on linen fabric with reading glasses, soft natural light
a coffee bag mockup with scattered beans on dark slate, artisanal coffee shop aesthetic
```

### Anlass- & Event Motive
Saisonale und thematische Visuals für jeden Anlass.

```
a christmas themed banner background with golden bokeh lights and pine branches, festive elegant
a summer sale visual with tropical leaves and sunglasses on bright yellow, energetic mood
a new year celebration image with champagne glasses and confetti, midnight blue and gold
```

### Wörter & Texteffekte
Typografie als visuelles Element – 3D, Material, Effekte.

```
the word SALE in 3D chrome letters with reflections, floating on gradient background
bold typography spelling SUMMER made of tropical flowers and leaves, white background
neon sign text reading OPEN glowing in pink and blue, dark brick wall background
```

### Hintergründe und Basismotive
Neutrale Grundlagen für Compositing und Layouts.

```
a soft gradient background transitioning from coral to lavender, smooth abstract
a textured concrete wall background with subtle cracks, industrial aesthetic, neutral gray
an out-of-focus nature background with green bokeh, fresh spring atmosphere
```

## Workflow-Tipp

Marketing-Assets oft in **Serien** produzieren:
- Gleichen Prompt mit Variationen (Farben, Jahreszeiten, Anlässe)
- Konsistente Bildsprache über Kampagne hinweg
- Basis-Motiv generieren, dann per Editing anpassen
